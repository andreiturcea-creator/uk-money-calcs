## What is the formula, in plain text?

Add up taxable turnover (everything not VAT-exempt, including zero-rated sales) for the last 12 months. If the total is over £90,000 you must register within 30 days of the end of that month. You must also register straight away if you expect more than £90,000 in the next 30 days alone.

## What rates and thresholds apply?

| Rule | 2026/27 |
|---|---|
| Registration threshold | £90,000 rolling 12 months |
| Forward-look test | more than £90,000 in the next 30 days alone |
| Voluntary registration | allowed below £90,000 |

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the VAT registration threshold?

£90,000 of taxable turnover. You must register if your total taxable turnover for the last 12 months goes over £90,000, or if you expect it to go over £90,000 in the next 30 days alone.

### Is it a rolling 12 months or the tax year?

It is a rolling 12 months, not the tax year or your accounting year. Check it at the end of every month.

### Can I register voluntarily below £90,000?

Yes. GOV.UK allows voluntary registration if your turnover is under £90,000.

### What counts as taxable turnover?

The total value of everything you sell that is not VAT exempt, including zero-rated sales.
