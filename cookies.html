UK Money Calcs does not set any cookies and does not use similar technologies such as local storage, tracking pixels or fingerprinting. There is no analytics or advertising code on the site, so there is no cookie banner.

If this ever changes, for example if we add analytics or ads, we will list every cookie here and ask for your consent before setting any that are not strictly necessary, as the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) require. See also our privacy notice.
