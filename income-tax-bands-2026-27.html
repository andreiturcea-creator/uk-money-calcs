FAQs

What are the Income Tax bands for 2026/27?

In England, Wales and Northern Ireland: Personal Allowance up to £12,570 at 0%, basic rate £12,571 to £50,270 at 20%, higher rate £50,271 to £125,140 at 40%, and additional rate over £125,140 at 45%.

What are employee National Insurance rates for 2026/27?

For category A employees: 8% on earnings between £242.01 and £967 a week (£1,048.01 to £4,189 a month), and 2% above £967 a week.

What happens to the Personal Allowance over £100,000?

It goes down by £1 for every £2 of adjusted net income over £100,000, so it is zero at £125,140 or above.

Are Scottish bands different?

Yes. Scotland has its own Income Tax bands; this page covers the rest of the UK.
