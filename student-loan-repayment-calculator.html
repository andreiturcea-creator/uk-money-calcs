FAQs

What are the student loan repayment thresholds?

GOV.UK currently shows yearly thresholds of £26,900 (Plan 1), £29,385 (Plan 2), £33,795 (Plan 4), £25,000 (Plan 5) and £21,000 (Postgraduate Loan). The page does not label these with a tax year, so check GOV.UK if you are planning for a later year.

How much do I repay?

9% of income over the threshold on Plans 1, 2, 4 and 5, and 6% over the threshold on a Postgraduate Loan.

I have a plan and a Postgraduate Loan. Do I pay both?

Yes. The Postgraduate Loan 6% is charged on top of the 9% for your undergraduate plan.

Does the amount I owe change my repayments?

No. Repayments depend only on your income, not on your balance.
