## What is the formula, in plain text?

SDLT is charged in slices: 0% on the first £125,000, 2% on £125,001–£250,000, 5% on £250,001–£925,000, 10% on £925,001–£1.5m and 12% above. First-time buyers (price up to £500,000): 0% up to £300,000 and 5% on £300,001–£500,000. Additional residential property: add 5 percentage points to every slice.
