**Short answer:** Pro rata salary = full-time salary × your hours ÷ full-time hours. A £30,000 full-time salary at 22.5 of 37.5 hours a week is £18,000 a year.

##

Holiday entitlement is pro-rated in the same way: 5.6 weeks × your days per week.

## What is the formula, in plain text?

Pro rata salary = full-time salary × (your hours per week ÷ full-time hours per week). The same works with days: full-time salary × (your days ÷ full-time days). Statutory holiday = 5.6 weeks × your working days per week.

## What rates and thresholds apply?

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Statutory holiday entitlement | 5.6 weeks a year (capped at 28 days) |

## What are some worked examples?

| Full-time salary | Full-time week | Your week | Pro rata salary |
|---|---|---|---|
| £30,000 | 37.5 hours | 22.5 hours | £18,000 |
| £25,000 | 5 days | 3 days | £15,000 |
| £42,000 | 40 hours | 30 hours | £31,500 |
| £36,000 | 37.5 hours | 15 hours | £14,400 |

Holiday example: 3 days a week × 5.6 = 16.8 days. The 5.6-week figure is long-standing statutory entitlement but was not part of tonight's GOV.UK rate check; confirm on GOV.UK.
