How is it calculated?

Personal allowance £12,570 (tapered by £1 per £2 above £100,000). Basic rate 20% to £50,270, higher 40% to £125,140, additional 45% above. Employee Class 1 NI: 8% between £12,570 and £50,270, 2% above. Thresholds are frozen until April 2031. Scotland uses different income tax bands, which this version does not cover yet.

What is the formula, in plain text?

Take-home = gross salary − pension (salary sacrifice) − Income Tax − employee National Insurance − student loan. Income Tax = 20% of income from £12,570 to £50,270 + 40% from £50,270 to £125,140 + 45% above (the £12,570 Personal Allowance falls by £1 for every £2 over £100,000). Employee NI = 8% of earnings from £12,570 to £50,270 + 2% above. Student loan = 9% (6% Postgraduate) of income above the plan threshold.
