How it works

Personal allowance £12,570 (tapered by £1 per £2 above £100,000). Basic rate 20% to £50,270, higher 40% to £125,140, additional 45% above. Employee Class 1 NI: 8% between £12,570 and £50,270, 2% above. Thresholds are frozen until April 2031. Scotland uses different income tax bands, which this version does not cover yet.
