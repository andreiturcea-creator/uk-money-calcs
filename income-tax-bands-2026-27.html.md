**Short answer:** For 2026/27 (6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027) in England, Wales and NI: Personal Allowance £12,570, basic rate 20% to £50,270, higher rate 40% to £125,140, additional rate 45% above. Employee NI is 8% then 2%, Class 4 NI 6% then 2%, and dividends are taxed at 10.75%, 35.75% and 39.35% after a £500 allowance.

## What are the Income Tax bands for 2026/27?

| Band | Taxable income | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Personal Allowance | Up to £12,570 | 0% |
| Basic | £12,571–£50,270 | 20% |
| Higher | £50,271–£125,140 | 40% |
| Additional | Over £125,140 | 45% |

## What are employee National Insurance rates for 2026/27?

| Weekly earnings | Monthly | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| £129–£242 | £559–£1,048 | 0% |
| £242.01–£967 | £1,048.01–£4,189 | 8% |
| Over £967 | Over £4,189 | 2% |

## What National Insurance do self-employed people pay?

Class 4: 6% on profits £12,570–£50,270, 2% above. Class 2 is treated as paid at profits of £7,105 or more; the voluntary rate is £3.65 a week.

## What are the dividend tax rates for 2026/27?

£500 allowance; 10.75% basic, 35.75% higher, 39.35% additional.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the Income Tax bands for 2026/27?

In England, Wales and Northern Ireland: Personal Allowance up to £12,570 at 0%, basic rate £12,571 to £50,270 at 20%, higher rate £50,271 to £125,140 at 40%, and additional rate over £125,140 at 45%.

### What are employee National Insurance rates for 2026/27?

For category A employees: 8% on earnings between £242.01 and £967 a week (£1,048.01 to £4,189 a month), and 2% above £967 a week.

### What happens to the Personal Allowance over £100,000?

It goes down by £1 for every £2 of adjusted net income over £100,000, so it is zero at £125,140 or above.

### Are Scottish bands different?

Yes. Scotland has its own Income Tax bands; this page covers the rest of the UK.
